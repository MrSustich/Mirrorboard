Mirrorboard Support

Mirrorboard is a split-screen whiteboard for one-on-one tutoring on iPad.

Need help? Email kyle.sustich@me.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Common questions

How do I set it up?
Lay your iPad flat on the table between you and your student, in landscape or portrait. You sit at the bottom edge (the green half); your student sits at the top edge (the pink half).

How do I make a perfect shape?
Tap the shapes button in the toolbar (a square with a circle) so it turns yellow. Draw a line, circle, triangle or box and hold the Pencil still at the end for a moment; it snaps into a neat shape.

How do I erase just part of a stroke?
Tap the eraser, then tap it again and choose Precise. To erase whole strokes at once, choose Whole strokes.

Can I move the toolbar?
Yes. Open Settings on the home screen and choose Left, Center or Right.

My finger doesn't draw.
Open Settings on the Mirrorboard home screen and turn off Pencil only. Also, right after you use Apple Pencil, finger touches on your half are ignored for a few seconds so your resting hand doesn't draw. Your student can still draw with a finger on their half.

How do I rename a board?
Open the board and tap Save in the palette. Change the title, student, or topic, then tap Save board.

How do I reorder boards?
On the home screen, press and hold a board, then drag it to a new spot.

Can I get my boards onto another iPad?
Boards are stored only on the iPad where you made them. They move to a new iPad when you restore that iPad from a backup of the old one.

How do I see the welcome tour again?
Open Settings on the home screen and tap Show intro.
