Privacy Policy

Mirrorboard · Last updated: October 26, 2026

In short
Mirrorboard does not collect, store, or share any of your data. Everything you create stays on your iPad.

What Mirrorboard stores

Mirrorboard saves the boards you create (drawings, board titles, student names, and topics) and your settings (default paper, Pencil only, toolbar position, and whether you've seen the welcome tour). All of this is stored only on your iPad, inside Mirrorboard's own storage.

What Mirrorboard collects

Nothing. Mirrorboard has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting of its own, and no third-party code that collects data. The app does not connect to the internet, so your boards and your students' names are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Backups

Like most apps, Mirrorboard's data is included in your iPad's regular backups (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer) if you have them turned on. Those backups are managed by Apple and by you, not by Mirrorboard, and we can't access them.

Deleting your data

You can delete a single board from the home screen with its trash button. To delete everything, delete the Mirrorboard app from your iPad.

Children's privacy

Mirrorboard is designed for teachers and tutors to use with students, including young children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, no information about children is collected or shared. If you prefer, you can use first names or initials when naming boards.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
